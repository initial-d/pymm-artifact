## Claim: rc11.cat forbids [x]=1 /\ [y]=1 for 2+2W attr-base.
Suppose G is an rc11-consistent execution of attr-base with x=1, y=1. Let G' be G restricted to
the events not on ox/oy. G' is a candidate execution of attrb-base: po restricted is attrb-base's
program order, and rf, co on x, y, mx, my are unchanged (no event on those locations is removed),
so the final values of x and y are unchanged. Every relation rc11.cat builds is defined positively
from po, rf, co, rmw, loc and event sets (rb = (rf^-1;mo)\id and sbl = sb\loc subtract only id
and loc, which restriction preserves), so each relation of G' is contained in the same relation of
G restricted to G''s events:
  sb, rf, mo, rmw, myrmw, rb, eco          restricted from G
  rs, sw, hb (= (sb|sw)+), hbl, sbl, scb   paths in G' are paths in G
  pscb (SC events of G' are SC events of G), pscf (empty: no fences)
Hence each axiom holds in G' because it holds in G:
  irreflexive hb;eco?        irreflexive (myrmw;eco)     empty (myrmw & (rb;mo))
  acyclic psc                acyclic (sb|rf)
and Dr is empty in both (all accesses are atomic: dr excludes A*A).
So G' is an rc11-consistent execution of attrb-base with x=1, y=1, contradicting the premise.
Therefore 2+2W attr-base is forbidden under rc11.cat.  Verdict: forbid (derived).
